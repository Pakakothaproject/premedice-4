## MedQA comparison

MedQA is an exam-style medical question benchmark. It measures multiple-choice medical knowledge, not clinical outcomes, safety, or real-patient performance.

| System | MedQA | Benchmark | Source |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Premedice 4 | 84.6% | Internal MedQA replication | Premedice internal, not peer-reviewed |
| Med-Gemini | 91.1% | MedQA | [Google Research, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18416) |
| Med-PaLM 2 | 86.5% | MedQA | [Nature, 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06291-2) |
| MedGemma 27B | 87.7% | MedQA | [Google, 2025](https://research.google/blog/medgemma-our-most-capable-open-models-for-health-ai-development/) |

## How to read this

- Scores are not directly comparable across different test sets or prompting methods.
- Exam accuracy does not prove diagnostic accuracy, safety, or better patient outcomes.
- Premedice's 84.6% is an internal replication and has not been peer-reviewed.
- The table is a directional comparison, not a regulatory claim.

## What would strengthen this page

A published, clinician-adjudicated evaluation with the case set, methodology, failures, and per-category results. Until then, this table should be read as a starting point, not a verdict.
